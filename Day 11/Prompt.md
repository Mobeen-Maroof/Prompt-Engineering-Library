# Day 11 – Prompt Chaining: Draft → Critique → Refine

## Objective

The objective of this exercise is to improve the quality of AI-generated content by splitting a complex task into multiple focused prompts instead of using a single prompt.

---

# Task

Summarize an article about Artificial Intelligence.

---

# Single Prompt

## Prompt

Summarize the following article in about 80 words.

## Observation

The AI generated a reasonable summary in one step, but there was no opportunity to review or improve the output before finalizing it.

---

# Prompt Chaining

## Step 1 – Draft

The AI created an initial summary.

---

## Step 2 – Critique

The summary was evaluated using the following rubric:

- Accuracy
- Completeness
- Clarity
- Grammar

Each criterion received a score and improvement suggestions.

---

## Step 3 – Refine

The AI rewrote the summary using the critique feedback.

The final version became clearer, more accurate, and easier to read.

---

# Comparison

| Method | Result |
|----------|--------|
| Single Prompt | Good summary produced in one step. |
| Prompt Chaining | Higher quality summary after draft, critique, and refinement. |

---

# What Did the Critique Catch?

The critique identified areas where the draft could be clearer and more concise. It also suggested improving grammar and ensuring all important points from the article were included. These improvements resulted in a stronger final summary.

---

# Conclusion

Prompt chaining improves AI output quality by dividing a task into smaller, focused steps. The Draft → Critique → Refine workflow allows the model to review and improve its own work before producing the final answer, making the output more reliable and polished.
