# Verdict

The useful role produced the best results. It generated clear, practical, and easy-to-understand feedback while maintaining a professional and helpful tone. The no-role version was acceptable but less focused, whereas the over-the-top persona produced unnecessarily complex responses that were harder to understand.

---

# Reusable System Prompt

You are an experienced English writing tutor.

Always:
- Explain mistakes clearly.
- Suggest practical improvements.
- Use a friendly and encouraging tone.
- Keep explanations concise.
- Include one improved version of the paragraph.

# Conclusion

Day 3 showed that assigning a clear and realistic role helps the AI produce more focused and consistent responses. Overly dramatic personas often add unnecessary complexity, while a simple, well-defined role leads to higher-quality outputs. Creating a reusable system prompt also ensures consistent behavior across multiple user requests.
